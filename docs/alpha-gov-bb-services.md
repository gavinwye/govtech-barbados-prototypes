# alpha.gov.bb services

Scraped from https://alpha.gov.bb/ on 2026-04-20. Organised by the six top-level sections on the live site. Descriptions marked (site) are taken directly from alpha.gov.bb; others are plain-English one-liners derived from the service title where the site itself provides no summary.

## Family, birth and relationships

- **Register a birth:** Record a newly born child with the Barbados civil registry.
- **Get a copy of a birth certificate:** Order an official copy of a birth certificate.
- **Register a death:** Record a death with the civil registry.
- **Get a copy of a death certificate:** Order an official copy of a death certificate.
- **Register a marriage:** Record a marriage with the civil registry.
- **Get a copy of a marriage certificate:** Order an official copy of a marriage certificate.
- **Marriage licences:** Apply for a licence to get married in Barbados.
- **Apply for a place at a day nursery:** Request a nursery place for your child.
