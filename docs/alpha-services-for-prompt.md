# alpha.gov.bb services not yet covered by a prototype

Services listed on alpha.gov.bb (scraped 2026-04-20) that are **not** already represented in the prototype catalog. Intended for inclusion in the chat system prompt so the assistant can refer citizens to the live alpha.gov.bb page for services we haven't built a prototype for.

Services dropped from this list because a prototype exists: Apply for a conductor licence, Apply for a driver's licence, Get a permit to play loud music, Apply for a licence to sell goods or services at a beach or park, Register a business name (handled by `caipo-business-names`).

## Family, birth and relationships

- **Register a birth:** Record a newly born child with the Barbados civil registry. https://alpha.gov.bb/family-birth-relationships/register-a-birth
- **Get a copy of a birth certificate:** Order an official copy of a birth certificate. https://alpha.gov.bb/family-birth-relationships/get-birth-certificate
- **Register a death:** Record a death with the civil registry. https://alpha.gov.bb/family-birth-relationships/register-a-death
- **Get a copy of a death certificate:** Order an official copy of a death certificate. https://alpha.gov.bb/family-birth-relationships/get-death-certificate
- **Register a marriage:** Record a marriage with the civil registry. https://alpha.gov.bb/family-birth-relationships/register-a-marriage
- **Get a copy of a marriage certificate:** Order an official copy of a marriage certificate. https://alpha.gov.bb/family-birth-relationships/get-marriage-certificate
- **Marriage licences:** Apply for a licence to get married in Barbados. https://alpha.gov.bb/family-birth-relationships/marriage-licences
- **Apply for a place at a day nursery:** Request a nursery place for your child. https://alpha.gov.bb/family-birth-relationships/apply-for-a-place-at-a-day-nursery

## Work and employment

- **Jobseekers:** Find a job, develop skills, or manage employment changes. https://alpha.gov.bb/work-employment/jobseekers
- **Apply to be a Project Protégé mentor:** Volunteer as a mentor on the Project Protégé programme. https://alpha.gov.bb/work-employment/apply-to-be-a-project-protege-mentor
- **Apply to the Barbados YouthADVANCE Corps (BYAC):** Join the BYAC youth development programme. https://alpha.gov.bb/work-employment/apply-to-the-barbados-youthadvance-corps
- **Community Sports Training Programme:** Register for a Youth Development Programme community sports course. https://alpha.gov.bb/work-employment/register-for-community-sports-training-programme
- **Register for a summer camp:** Sign a child up for a government summer camp. https://alpha.gov.bb/work-employment/register-summer-camp
- **Apply to volunteer at a sports camp:** Offer your time as a sports camp volunteer. https://alpha.gov.bb/work-employment/apply-to-volunteer-at-a-sports-camp
- **Apply for a position as a temporary teacher:** Apply to teach on a short-term basis in a state school. https://alpha.gov.bb/work-employment/apply-for-a-position-as-a-temporary-teacher
- **Apply to the Job Start Plus programme:** Join the Job Start Plus employment scheme. https://alpha.gov.bb/work-employment/apply-to-jobstart-plus-programme

## Money and financial support

- **Apply for financial assistance:** If you live in Barbados and need financial help you can apply for financial assistance. https://alpha.gov.bb/money-financial-support/apply-financial-assistance
- **EZPay:** Pay government bills and fees online. https://alpha.gov.bb/money-financial-support/ezpay
- **Tax online:** File and manage your taxes online. https://alpha.gov.bb/money-financial-support/tax-online
- **Get disaster relief assistance:** Apply for support if you've been affected by a disaster. https://alpha.gov.bb/money-financial-support/get-disaster-relief-assistance
- **Get a Primary School Textbook Grant:** Apply for help with the cost of primary school textbooks. https://alpha.gov.bb/money-financial-support/get-a-primary-school-textbook-grant

## Travel, ID and citizenship

- **Apply for a passport:** Get a Barbados passport for international travel. https://alpha.gov.bb/travel-id-citizenship/apply-for-a-passport
- **Visa information:** Find out about visas for entering Barbados. https://alpha.gov.bb/travel-id-citizenship/visa-information
- **Visitor permit application:** Apply for permission to stay in Barbados as a visitor. https://alpha.gov.bb/travel-id-citizenship/visitor-permit-application
- **Medical requirements:** Check the health documents needed to enter Barbados. https://alpha.gov.bb/travel-id-citizenship/medical-requirements
- **National registration:** Register for a national ID. https://alpha.gov.bb/travel-id-citizenship/national-registration
- **Getting around Barbados:** Information on transport around the island. https://alpha.gov.bb/travel-id-citizenship/getting-around-barbados
- **Local information:** General information for residents and visitors. https://alpha.gov.bb/travel-id-citizenship/local-information
- **Ports of Entry:** Details of airports and seaports you can use to enter the country. https://alpha.gov.bb/travel-id-citizenship/ports-of-entry
- **Get a document notarised:** Have a document officially certified. https://alpha.gov.bb/travel-id-citizenship/get-a-document-notarised
- **Redirect my personal mail:** Ask the Post Office to forward your personal mail. https://alpha.gov.bb/travel-id-citizenship/post-office-redirection-individual
- **Redirect my business mail:** Ask the Post Office to forward mail for your business. https://alpha.gov.bb/travel-id-citizenship/post-office-redirection-business
- **Tell the Post Office someone has died:** Notify the Post Office that a person has died so their mail can be handled. https://alpha.gov.bb/travel-id-citizenship/post-office-redirection-deceased
